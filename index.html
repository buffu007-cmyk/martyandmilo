Is your dog a fussy eater?

Our Story

I'm a support worker from Bath. A few years back, I got a dog — Milo. He was great, but he had one problem: he was incredibly fussy about eating.

We were going through endless bags of food, changing his diet every few months, wasting money because he'd refuse to eat what we gave him. Sarah and I were frustrated — we knew he was hungry, but he just wouldn't touch his food.

So I decided to build something. A simple system that enhances the smell of his food, making it irresistible. I tested it on Milo, and it worked. Now he eats everything we put in front of him — even when he normally refuses.

Marley & Milo is that solution. It's designed for dogs like Milo — fussy eaters who just need a little sensory help to get excited about food again.

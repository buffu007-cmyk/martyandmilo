Is your dog a fussy eater?

Our Story

Milo is a rescue with a big personality and a very fussy appetite. We tried endless bags of food and changed his diet again and again, wasting money on meals he'd walk away from. We knew he was hungry, he just wasn't interested.

So I built something to make his food more tempting. I tested it on Milo, and now he gets excited about his food again.

Marley & Milo is named after my two dogs: Marley, my big gentle shadow who we lost too young, and Milo, the fussy one who inspired all this. It's made for dogs like him who just need a little help to get excited about food again.
